# Project spec (positions labelled)

Objective (RATIFIED, 20 Oct 2026; full text and reasoning in PIPELINE.md §1):

**A Vedāntic rendering of the Bible.** Purpose: to bring the Bible into humanity's moral dialogue as a participant, not as humanity's exclusive moral constitution. What: the complete Bible (66 books), rendered from the Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek originals into Sanskrit anuṣṭubh, with a faithful mūla and labelled bhāṣyas. How: Vedānta is the medium of meaning; read through pūrvapakṣa and uttarapakṣa, the Old Testament is the ground understood on its own terms, and the New Testament its reinterpretation through the life, teachings, death and resurrection of Jesus. Key: Jesus, read as Guru from the texts and apart from any church's vested agenda; born outside inherited status, a ṛṣi by conduct; his teaching tests whether any identity has become an excuse for abandoning moral responsibility. Discipline: faithful text, context-true commentary; the polemic charge answered in commentary, never by altering the text; every gap disclosed; every bridge labelled by owner; AI's study of each civilisation acknowledged.


Ratified rules:
1. Accurate, rhythmic anuṣṭubh. Poetic excellence is not the objective. Metre checked against corpora (Ramayana, Gita, Durga Saptashati).
2. Maker-reviewer: different model vendors draft and review.
3. Divine names and core untranslatables kept as sound, not translated.
4. Idol and polemic verses stand as written in the mūla. Bhāṣya per passage: target, internal reform, reception history, Vedāntic response (arcāvatāra).
5. The project lead's role starts after the machine passes: taste and ratification.
6. Structural boilerplate (uvāca, colophons, frames, phalaśruti) derived from Indic scripture.
7. Lawyer's posture: faithful text, context-true commentary.
8. Source gender is authentic and kept in the mūla: spiritual terms keep the grammatical gender they have in the source scripture (rūakh feminine, logos masculine), and all agreeing words follow. Sanskrit equivalents and gender analysis go in the bhāṣya (20 Oct 2026; hybrid 'Sanskrit word for concepts' rule NOT ratified).

Review handling (proposed, for ratification):
- Reviewer output, from any model, is a finding. It is never a decision and never the project's position.
- Maker fixes verifiable errors directly: grammar, metre, agreement.
- Any interpretive or terminological change is logged as a proposal with status PENDING RATIFICATION.
- Model reviewers share blind spots; human expert gates remain.

Proposed (not ratified):
- Loanwords limited to names and core untranslatables, each with one glossary entry.
- Opening invocation drawn from the Bible's own words.
- Independent human gates: Hebrew, Greek, Sanskrit chandas/Vedānta reviewers. Full review of pilot, then 5% sample per book.

9. Pronunciation of kept words (20 Oct 2026): Hebrew per the Tiberian pointing of the source text; Greek per 1st-century Koine. Nukta scheme (ח ह़, כ ख़, ע to be fixed), short ĕ/ŏ (ऎ/ऒ) where the source vowel is short, stress marked with U+0951 on the stressed syllable. The Name is written in fixed form and read Adonai. Each kept word gets a licensed native/liturgical audio reference.

Bhāṣya themes: gender of spiritual vocabulary as a worldview; its resonance with the Deva-Asura conflict over spiritual concepts.

Open decisions: see register/terms.yaml entries marked DECISION; licence for this rendering.
